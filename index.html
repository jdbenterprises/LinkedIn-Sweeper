📋 Research checklist

Every lead becomes a useful brief.

  • ✅ LinkedIn bio, headline, hiring posts, founder comments, and recent activity
  • ✅ Brand website positioning, lead magnets, case studies, offers, and conversion gaps
  • ✅ Public email patterns, newsletter pages, directories, and compliant enrichment sources
  • ✅ Recent launches, events, grants, partnerships, roles, podcast appearances, and press

Opportunity lens

A founder posting consistently but sending traffic to a vague homepage → offer a sharper conversion path and signature offer audit.

A growing team hiring managers quickly → suggest a leadership operating rhythm workshop before culture becomes accidental.

A startup celebrating funding but lacking customer education → draft a market-education content system and community flywheel.

A creative brand with strong visuals but weak retention → propose customer journey mapping, loyalty loops, and referral rituals.
